# Chris Leon – Hermes Assistant SMS

The Hermes Assistant is a personal, low-volume messaging service operated by Chris Leon. It sends requested personal-assistant notifications, reminders, status updates, and two-way responses. It is not used for marketing or lead generation.

## How to opt in

Enrollment is available only to people personally invited by Chris Leon.

To confirm that you want to receive messages, text **START** to **(636) 210-5525** from the mobile number you want enrolled.

By texting START, you consent to receive automated informational and conversational messages from the Chris Leon – Hermes Assistant. Message frequency varies based on your requests and interactions with the service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to unsubscribe or **HELP** for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

## Confirmation message

After opting in, you will receive a message similar to:

> Chris Leon – Hermes Assistant: You are subscribed to requested assistant notifications and two-way replies. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

For help, email [raven82neo@proton.me](mailto:raven82neo@proton.me).
