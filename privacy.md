# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chris Leon handles information associated with the **Chris Leon – Hermes Assistant** SMS program.

## Information collected

When you enroll in or communicate with the Hermes Assistant, the program may collect:

- Your mobile telephone number.
- Records of your consent and opt-out requests.
- The contents of messages you exchange with the service.
- Technical and delivery information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

## How information is used

This information is used only to provide requested personal-assistant notifications, reminders, status updates, and two-way responses; maintain consent and opt-out records; prevent misuse; and operate or improve the service.

## Mobile-information sharing

Mobile information, including telephone numbers and SMS consent records, will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process information solely as necessary to transmit messages, operate the Hermes Assistant, provide technical infrastructure, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with law. SMS consent records are not shared for purposes unrelated to providing the messaging service.

## Messaging choices

Message frequency varies according to your requests and interactions with the service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply **HELP** for assistance or email [raven82neo@proton.me](mailto:raven82neo@proton.me).
